Help Guides

Everything you need to get started and make the most of RelicRoute. Choose your platform below.

Getting Started

  1. Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Search tab and type “RelicRoute”.
  3. Tap Get next to the RelicRoute app (look for our green and bronze logo).
  4. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password if prompted.
  5. Once installed, tap Open to launch the app.

Requirements: iOS 16.0 or later. Compatible with iPhone and iPad.

  1. Open RelicRoute and tap Create Account.
  2. You can sign up using your email address or Sign in with Apple.
  3. Enter your name and create a password (minimum 8 characters).
  4. Verify your email by tapping the link sent to your inbox.
  5. Complete the onboarding by selecting your preferred historical eras and find types.

RelicRoute needs location access to show nearby detecting spots and provide accurate recommendations.

  1. When prompted, tap “Allow While Using App” for location access.
  2. If you previously denied access, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > RelicRoute and select “While Using the App”.

Tip: “While Using the App” is recommended for the best balance of functionality and battery life.

Using the Map

  1. Pan: Drag with one finger to move around the map.
  2. Zoom: Pinch with two fingers to zoom in and out.
  3. Centre on Location: Tap the location arrow icon in the bottom-right corner.
  4. View Spot Details: Tap any highlighted grid square to see its Detectability Score and data layers.

Use the layer control to customise which data is shown on the map:

  1. Tap the Layers icon (stacked squares) in the top-right of the map.
  2. Toggle layers on or off:
    • LiDAR Terrain — Shows elevation anomalies and hidden earthworks.
    • Historic OS Maps — Overlays georeferenced historical Ordnance Survey maps.
    • PAS Finds — Displays Portable Antiquities Scheme find density.
    • Scheduled Monuments — Highlights protected areas (no-go zones).
    • Soil Data — Shows soil pH and drainage information.
  3. Tap anywhere outside the panel to close it.

Each 1km grid square receives a personalised Detectability Score (0–100) based on your preferences.

  • 80–100 (Gold): Excellent — high density of relevant historical indicators.
  • 60–79 (Bronze): Good — promising data across multiple layers.
  • 40–59 (Green): Moderate — some indicators present.
  • Below 40: Low — limited data for your chosen eras/types.

Tap any grid square for a detailed breakdown of which data layers contributed to the score.

Preferences & Settings

  1. Tap the Profile icon in the tab bar.
  2. Select Detecting Preferences.
  3. Choose your preferred historical eras (e.g. Roman, Medieval, Georgian, Victorian).
  4. Select your target find types (e.g. coins, jewellery, artefacts, military).
  5. Tap Save. Your map scores will update to reflect your new preferences.
  1. Go to Profile > Notification Settings.
  2. Toggle notifications for:
    • New Spot Alerts — Get notified when high-scoring spots appear near you.
    • Data Updates — Know when new LiDAR or PAS data is added to your area.
    • Weekly Digest — A summary of top spots in your region.

You can also manage notifications in iOS Settings > Notifications > RelicRoute.

Account & Subscription

  1. Tap Profile > Upgrade to Pro.
  2. Review the Pro features and pricing.
  3. Tap Subscribe and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password.
  4. Your subscription is managed through your Apple ID. To cancel or change, go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions.
  1. Go to Profile > Account Settings > Delete Account.
  2. Confirm by entering your password.
  3. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

If you have an active Pro subscription, cancel it first through Apple’s subscription management to avoid future charges.
