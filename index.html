Finding Good Ground Shouldn’t Take Longer Than Detecting It.

Scouting the right location takes hours of cross-referencing old maps, checking legal boundaries, and guessing where history actually happened.

RelicRoute changes everything. We’ve built the UK’s most advanced detecting recommendation engine. By combining seven layers of open data into a single, easy-to-use mobile app, we give you personalized, highly-scored spot recommendations based on your favourite historical eras and find types.

RelicRoute is a mobile application available on iOS and Android that provides AI-powered metal detecting location recommendations across the United Kingdom. It analyses DEFRA LiDAR data, Historic Ordnance Survey maps, the Portable Antiquities Scheme (PAS) database, and British Geological Survey (BGS) soil data to generate personalised Detectability Scores for 1km grid squares. The app also displays legal boundaries including Scheduled Monuments, SSSIs, and National Parks.

Find the best land to seek permissions for, and never waste a weekend on a dead field again.

Why RelicRoute?

X-Ray the Landscape with LiDAR

See what the naked eye can’t. Our AI scans 1-meter resolution DEFRA LiDAR data to automatically flag hidden terrain anomalies — like ancient earthworks, flattened burial mounds, ditches, and long-lost enclosures.

Detect with Absolute Confidence

Never accidentally stray onto protected land. RelicRoute clearly highlights the strict legal boundaries of Scheduled Monuments, SSSIs, and National Parks. Stay on the right side of the law and protect our shared heritage.

Time-Travel with Historic Maps & PAS Data

Overlay georeferenced Historic OS Maps to reveal lost pathways, forgotten farmsteads, and old field boundaries. Plus, we integrate the Portable Antiquities Scheme database, showing you the density and eras of previous finds in your area.

Know Your Ground Before You Dig

Is the ground heavily acidic? Will it be waterlogged? RelicRoute pulls BGS Soil Data to give you instant readouts on soil pH and drainage, helping you predict the preservation quality of your targets and the digging conditions.
