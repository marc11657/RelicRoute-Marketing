2. Description of Service

RelicRoute is a mobile application that provides AI-powered metal detecting location recommendations across the United Kingdom. The Service analyses publicly available data including DEFRA LiDAR, Historic Ordnance Survey maps, the Portable Antiquities Scheme (PAS) database, and British Geological Survey (BGS) soil data.

3. User Responsibilities

You are responsible for:

  • Obtaining landowner permission before metal detecting on any land.
  • Complying with all applicable laws, including the Treasure Act 1996 and the Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Areas Act 1979.
  • Respecting Scheduled Monuments, SSSIs, and other protected areas. Metal detecting on protected land without consent is a criminal offence.
  • Reporting any treasure finds to your local coroner within 14 days, as required by law.
